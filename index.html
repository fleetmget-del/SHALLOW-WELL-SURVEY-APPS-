Formula Guide
15. Volume per Shallow Well Normal Level × 200 L
Uses the normal water level to estimate the well volume.
16. Total Daily Water Need Population × 30 L per person/day
Estimates the daily water requirement of the people using the well.
17. Current Supply of Shallow Wells Current Level × 200 L
Estimates the available water based on the current water level.
Well Recharge Needed by Tabay Normal Level − Current Level = Recharge Needed (Tabay)
If the current level is lower than the normal level, this shows how many Tabay are needed to recharge the well back to normal. If the current level is already normal or higher, the result is 0 Tabay.
18. Available Water Supply Current Supply − Total Daily Water Need
Positive result means enough supply; negative result means shortage.
19. Status of Water Sufficiency If Available Supply ≥ 0 = Sufficient; otherwise = Insufficient
Automatically checks if the current supply can cover the daily need.
20. Individuals Affected If Insufficient = Population; if Sufficient = 0
Counts affected individuals when the water supply is insufficient.

Settings, Offline App, and Online Sync

No sync URL saved yet.
Offline mode: GPS, the Iloilo City map, barangay detection, and saving records all work with no internet. Online mode: records waiting to upload are sent to Google Sheets automatically when internet returns (or press Sync Now). Every record has a permanent Record ID, so many phones can upload to the same sheet without duplicate rows. Check and rate records in the sheet's Checked By / Rating / Checker Remarks columns.
